## Method notes

- Polarity = (1 - w) * VADER compound + w * tanh(lexicon weight sum), with w = `sentiment.lexicon_weight` = 0.5. VADER available: True.
- Label band: +/-0.05 (VADER's convention). Negation: a negator within 3 tokens flips a term and damps it by 0.75.
- Missing VADER would downgrade the method to lexicon-only rather than fail the run; that is recorded in `scoring.method`.
- Sarcasm is not modelled. `sent_sarcasm_share` reports how much of the cell carries a marker, so the limit is visible on the card instead of being argued about later.
- Attention z-scores compare day d with days strictly before it (`shift(1)`); forward returns are null, not wrapped, in the last h sessions.

## Structural limits, restated

- One regime only (2021-09-30 -> 2022-09-29, 252 sessions): nothing here generalises beyond it.
- The independent unit is the ticker-day, not the tweet. 80,793 tweets are not 80,793 observations.
- TSLA is 46.3% of the corpus, so a pooled statistic is nearly a TSLA statistic; pooled results are always shown with a TSLA-excluded variant.
- 28.5% of rows are cross-posted text; AMZN, MSFT, PG, F and NOC are 100% cross-posted and are descriptive-only (tier C).
- 55.8% of rows are promo-shaped and most contain a t.co link.
- ~49% of tweets roll to the next session (after the 16:00 ET close or on a non-trading date).
- There is no author field, so author-level, bot-detection and unique-user metrics are impossible, not deferred.
- A tweet's timestamp is not the time the market learned the news.

These are limits of the dataset, not of the pipeline. `supported` in a finding means
"passed this demo's checks" and nothing stronger; nothing here is trading advice.
